MTN SME Excellence Awards 2026

12 Aug 2026
In Person, Cape Town
340 attended
Watch the Replay · 42 min
340Attendees
12SME finalists
5Categories awarded

How it went

The MTN SME Excellence Awards celebrated the standout South African small businesses of the year across five categories, from Best New Business to Township Enterprise of the Year. Finalists pitched to a panel of judges from MTN Business and SME South Africa, with winners announced on the night.

Kevinot Farming, this year's SME JumpStarter winner, was among the businesses recognised, alongside finalists from Gauteng, the Western Cape, and KwaZulu-Natal.
