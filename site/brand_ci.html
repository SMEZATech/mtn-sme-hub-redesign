Corporate Identity

Same brand. Better structure.

The reference for the MTN SME Hub redesign: the exact colour, type, and component rules the mockups must be held to. This is not a new brand. It is MTN's real corporate identity, documented so the redesign can't drift.

Primary · MTN Yellow #FFC502 Headings · MTN Brighter Sans XBold Body · MTN Brighter Sans Regular Ink · rgb(33,37,41)

How to use this document

One reference, held constant

Every value on this page was pulled from the live site's computed styles, not guessed or reinterpreted. Where the redesign proposes something new, like a neutral scale, a component, or a nav pattern, it's marked Proposed. Everything else is Locked: MTN's actual identity, not ours to change.

Redesign scope

What actually changes

The brand palette is locked, so this redesign is not a colour refresh. It's an information architecture and hierarchy upgrade. Same yellow, same ink, same logo, restructured around how people actually browse a resource hub.

Today: blog layout
Nav: Home / Business Advice / Insights only
5 content pillars exist only as homepage tiles
Articles: no date, no author, no read time
Same articles repeated across multiple homepage tabs
Insights sub-sections (Guides/Templates/L&D/Events) buried one level deep
Redesign: publication layout
Nav: pillar-aware, all 5 categories + Insights sub-nav surfaced
Pillars become primary browsing structure, not decoration
Articles carry date + read time as standard metadata
Cross-tagged content de-duplicated, shown once per surface
Insights promoted to a first-class nav destination

Contrast rule: the one mistake this brand invites

MTN Yellow at full saturation fails text contrast against white. It looks bright and "on-brand" but is close to unreadable as body or link text. Yellow is a fill and accent colour, never a text colour on a light background. Use ink text on a yellow fill, or a darkened shade of yellow for small label text only.

Yellow text on white Contrast ≈ 1.6:1 Fails WCAG AA
Ink text on yellow fill Contrast ≈ 11:1 Passes AAA
Yellow on ink Contrast ≈ 7:1 Passes AA

  • Don't substitute a condensed or narrow face for Brighter Sans

03 · Logo (Locked)

Logo usage

The live site uses a black wordmark lockup (logo-black.png). Keep clear space equal to the cap-height of the "M" on all sides; never recolour, stretch, or place it directly on a busy photo without a scrim.

✓ Do
  • Use the black lockup on white or light surfaces
  • Use a white-knockout version on ink or photo backgrounds
  • Maintain minimum clear space around the mark
✕ Don't
  • Don't use the yellow-fill logo except on ink/dark or photo backgrounds
  • Don't recolour, rotate, or add effects to the mark
  • Don't crowd it: no UI element inside the clear-space zone

07 · Business Solutions (Proposed)

Real products, no photography, never flat

MTN Business plans and Managed Security Solutions don't have product photography, but a plain white card reads as an afterthought. The fix is a yellow-infused surface treatment: a soft radial glow, a gradient edge, and an arrow affordance instead of a button, since these link through to a configurator or lead form rather than an instant purchase.

09 · Global rules

What holds every screen accountable

✓ Do
  • Ink text on white/surface, ink text on yellow fills
  • One accent colour, yellow, used with restraint
  • Pillar identity via labels/tags, not a rainbow of category colours
  • Every article card carries date + read time
  • Nav always exposes the 5 pillars, Insights sub-nav, and Business Solutions
  • Business Solutions cards use the yellow-glow treatment: never flat, never a stock photo
✕ Don't
  • Don't invent secondary brand colours to "add variety"
  • Don't use yellow as body or link text on light backgrounds
  • Don't restructure the 5 pillars or rename them
  • Don't let a mockup screen ship a value not on this page
