Complete Guide to Registering a Business in SA

MTN SME Hub Editorial
· Updated 6 Aug 2026 · 12 min read · 7 steps
What you'll need before you start
  • · Your South African ID or passport (for non-citizens)
  • · A proposed company name, plus two backup options
  • · A registered address for the business
  • · Around R175 and 5 to 10 working days

Registering a business in South Africa is more straightforward than most first-time founders expect, provided you follow the steps in order. This guide walks through the full process, from reserving your company name to getting your first tax number, using CIPC's own current process.

1

Reserve your company name

Submit up to four proposed names to CIPC for approval. Names are checked for uniqueness and against restricted words, so have a backup or two ready in case your first choice is taken.

2

Choose your business structure

Most new SMEs register as a Private Company (Pty Ltd), which limits personal liability and is what most funders and clients expect to see. Sole proprietorship is simpler but offers no liability protection.

3

Register with CIPC

File your registration through the CIPC online portal. You'll need your approved name, ID details, and a registered address. Standard processing takes 5 to 10 working days.

  • Certified ID copy uploaded
  • Registration fee paid
4

Get your registration certificate and number

Once approved, CIPC issues your Certificate of Incorporation and a unique registration number. This is what banks, funders, and clients will ask for as proof your business exists.

5

Register for tax with SARS

CIPC registration automatically triggers a SARS tax reference number in most cases. Confirm this on eFiling, and register for VAT separately once you cross the R1 million turnover threshold.

6

Open a business bank account

You'll need your Certificate of Incorporation, ID, and proof of address. Keeping business banking separate from personal accounts from day one makes bookkeeping, tax, and future funding applications far easier.

7

Register as an employer, if you're hiring

If you plan to hire staff, register for PAYE and UIF with SARS before your first payday. See our guide on setting up payroll for the full process.
