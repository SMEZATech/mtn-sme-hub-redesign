MTN SMEHub Marketplace: a delivery proposal

A proposal for evolving SMEHub into a marketplace built on three pillars: Shop, Sales Advisor and Portal, with two ways to deliver it.

SMEHub Marketplace
Shop, Sales Advisor, Portal
Executive summary

What we're proposing

A SMEHub Marketplace built around three pillars: Shop, Sales Advisor and Portal, giving every visitor a place to browse, get guided to the right product, and stay connected to MTN over time. We have already built working screens for all three against the existing SMEHub site, so the concept can be seen and clicked through rather than described in the abstract. This document sets out that shared scope, then two ways to deliver it. Cost and timeline figures are marked TBC pending a short scoping conversation on team composition and which partner integrations come first.

Prototype

What's already built

Four working sections show the marketplace concept in practice, built on the existing SMEHub site so the transition reads as an evolution rather than a rebuild.

ShopA self-service catalogue with a monthly deals band and a clear call to action on every product: Buy Now for plans and devices, Get a Quote for managed services.
Sales AdvisorA short survey that produces a tailored recommendation, a next step, and a calendar slot to book a consultation.
PortalFree membership with sign-up and login, a member dashboard showing activity, funding opportunities and rewards, and a controlled path to becoming a customer.
Partner EcosystemFive categories of partner, from funders to learning platforms, each with a defined journey inside the marketplace.
Common scope

What both options deliver

Both delivery options are scoped against the same three pillars. What differs between them is who builds and runs each piece, not what gets built.

Shop

Self-service browsing to checkout

Devices, SIM-only, FWA and existing Made For Business plans organised by job/category, with vetting introduced near checkout and context retained into the CloudBlue provisioning flow for complex products.

Sales Advisor

Guided intent to action

Public solutions browsing with trackable actions, a progressive question survey, a predictive next-best-action, calendar scheduling with a product and sales advisor, and recovery of abandoned or unsuccessful journeys.

Portal

An ongoing relationship

Free login and SMEHub membership, profile and engagement history visible to MTN and the member, rewards and funding opportunities, controlled conversion from subscriber to customer, and API access to partner products.

Foundations

What sits underneath

One SME profile spanning content, Shop and Portal, with CloudBlue retained as the subscription and billing engine for authenticated SaaS rather than replaced. Each partner integration is scoped individually, since some need subscriber-base access, some a redirect link, and some just a content feature.

1

Full Agency Delivery

SME South Africa owns the full build, end to end: scope, architecture, implementation, and the operating model that keeps it running.

Scope, architecture and approach

Design, front-end build, and content operations delivered by SME South Africa on the existing MTN SME Hub codebase. Portal authentication, the Sales Advisor recommendation logic, and partner API integrations are the pieces most likely to need a backend/platform partner brought in under this agency's management, rather than built in-house; this needs a technical scoping conversation before it's estimated.

Shared delivery approach

SME South Africa leads design and front-end delivery and coordinates a shared backlog with MTN and any technical partner brought in for authentication and the recommendation engine. Regular joint checkpoints track integration dependencies rather than a single agency-owned critical path.
